Learn AI · teach AI · keep the skill

        
Learn it, teach it back, and take it with you.

        
Brainstem is the bare twin; OpenRappter is the fully built-out twin. They share the exact `/chat` wire while keeping separate identity, state, and worker ports. Chat is the control surface. GitHub Copilot teaches by doing: it can build and hotload an agent, drive the visible application, test the result, and preserve the skill as a portable RAPP capability.

      

      

        

          

            
What is this for?

            
Tell Copilot the outcome you need. It helps you learn the pattern, turns that learning into a single-file capability, hotloads it beside the global agents, and proves it through the real OpenRappter chat.

            
It is the fastest path from an idea to evidence. It is not the final production host.

          

          

            
Who should use it?

            
Technical sellers, architects, specialists, engineers, partners, and customer builders who can steer a prototype and judge whether it solves the business problem.

            
It is not the default interface for account executives or end users. They consume the validated outcome in the right production experience.

          

          

            
When should I reach for it?

            
    
              
  • A customer asks, "Can AI do this process?"
    • 
              
  • The requirements are still conversational or ambiguous.
    • 
              
  • You need a demo, feasibility proof, or process specification quickly.
    • 
              
  • You want failures to become concrete evidence Copilot can fix.
    • 
              
  • You need portable agent files that teammates can hot-load and improve.
    • 
            

          

          

            
When should I not use it?

            
    
              
  • Simple employee knowledge or Work IQ tasks already fit Scout.
    • 
              
  • The production design, integrations, governance, and target are already known.
    • 
              
  • You need to put real customer data into an unapproved local prototype.
    • 
              
  • You are treating a local proof as a production-ready solution.
    • 
            

          

          

            
How the tools fit together

            

              
ScoutUse for accessible employee workflows, Work IQ, knowledge, and shareable skills when the experience already fits.

              
GitHub CopilotUse the built-in Rappter Surgeon panel as the full builder: create, test, diagnose, repair, and operate agents without leaving this window. VS Code remains optional.

              
OpenRappterUse the full local application as the proving ground: hot-load capabilities, inspect tool calls, and keep the resulting RAPP.

              
Copilot Studio / FoundryUse for governed production delivery, enterprise identity, connectors, telemetry, scale, and customer handoff.

            

          

          

            
The rapid customer loop

            
    
              
  1. Frame: capture the problem, users, inputs, outputs, constraints, and success proof.
    2. 
              
  2. Delegate: chat with OpenRappter; let the AI load the smallest useful agent set.
    3. 
              
  3. Watch: let the agent operate the real interface with visible cursor, click, and typing feedback.
    4. 
              
  4. Correct: tell the built-in Rappter Surgeon to inspect the failure, fix it, and retest while you watch.
    5. 
              
  5. Package: share the agents, demo, evidence, requirements, and recommended production route.
    6. 
            

          

          

            
What should come out?

            
    
              
  • A working, evidence-backed local prototype.
    • 
              
  • Portable single-file agents that document the process deterministically.
    • 
              
  • Known gaps for data, authentication, connectors, security, and governance.
    • 
              
  • A demo story plus design/SOW-ready requirements.
    • 
              
  • A clear route to Scout, Copilot Studio, Foundry, custom code, or "do not proceed."
    •

Start with this prompt — or show it instead

            
I am working on a rapid customer proof. Interview me until you understand the business problem, users, inputs, expected outputs, constraints, and what would prove success. Then propose the smallest single-file RAPP agent set that can test the idea locally with synthetic or approved data. Build it, run it through OpenRappter, show me the evidence, identify production gaps, and recommend the right production host. Do not call the prototype production-ready.

            

              Can't describe it? Show it. Show Mode turns a live recording, a video, screenshots, or a transcript into an agent you test here first, then promote. Preview the click-through — nothing runs live.
